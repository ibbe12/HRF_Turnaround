Leave balance

Annual Leave 30 days (32 days once the person has 5 or more years of service), FRL 10, SL 15 and SLM 15 per leave year. Off Day (one per week) and Release (unlimited) are recorded but never deducted from any balance. Each person's leave year starts on the anniversary of their join date, so enter join dates below. Fridays are not deducted from Annual Leave; all other days are counted as calendar days. Leave records saved before this update count as Annual Leave.

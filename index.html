Import from attendance machine

Export the punch / attendance report from the machine software as a CSV or Excel file, then choose it here. The machine ID is matched to the SN number, so 369 becomes SN-0369. The first punch of each day becomes Check-In and the last punch becomes Check-Out.

If date and time are in one column, choose that column for both Date and Time.

Add leave

Staff on leave are automatically excluded when the flight date falls in their leave period.

Leave balance

Annual Leave 30 days (32 days once the person has 5 or more years of service), FRL 10, SL 15 and SLM 15 per leave year. Off Day (one per week) and Release (unlimited) are recorded but never deducted from any balance. Each person's leave year starts on the anniversary of their join date, so enter join dates below. Fridays and public holidays are not deducted from Annual Leave; all other days are counted as calendar days. Leave records saved before this update count as Annual Leave.
